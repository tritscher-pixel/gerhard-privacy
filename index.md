# Privacy Policy / Datenschutzerklärung

**App:** Orbitalis

---

## English

### 1. Data Collection and Storage
Orbitalis does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All game progress, settings, and high scores are saved locally on your device only and are never transmitted to external servers.

### 2. Third-Party Services
This app does not use any third-party analytics tools, tracking services, or advertising networks.

### 3. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via email.

Diese App nutzt keine Analyse-Tools, Tracking-Dienste oder Werbenetzwerke von Drittanbietern.
